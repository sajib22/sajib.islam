What an in-building system looks like

Distributed Antenna System (DAS)

A DAS extends wireless coverage inside buildings where the outdoor macro network cannot penetrate. Instead of relying on a single high-power source, it distributes the signal through a network of low-power antennas spread across every floor.

How it works: A carrier base station feeds into a multi-network combiner at ground level. From there, the combined signal travels up a vertical riser through couplers on each floor. Each coupler connects to splitters that distribute the signal to ceiling-mounted antennas, delivering uniform coverage across the entire floor plate.

Why it matters: Modern buildings with thick concrete, low-E glass and dense floor plans block most outdoor signal. A properly designed DAS ensures reliable voice, data and emergency service coverage throughout the venue — from parking garages to penthouse floors.
