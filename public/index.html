What I Do

In-Building Design

DAS, IBS and Private 5G for venues a macro network cannot reach. Design packages, link budgets and coverage predictions that match what the building actually delivers.

Commissioning and Test

Head-end and remote integration across ADRF, Corning/BTI and Zinwave. CW, PIM and walk testing, EMF and Safety Code 6 validation, and the close-out pack that proves it.

RAN Optimization

Fifteen years of worst-cell tuning, mobility and handover troubleshooting, and multi-vendor KPI mapping across Nokia, Ericsson and Huawei estates.

Analysis Automation

Mapping and Documentation
